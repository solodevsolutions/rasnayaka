About Rasnayaka Ayurveda

EN සිං

Founded in 1995 by Dr. Nayani Rasnayaka, our hospital combines centuries-old Ayurvedic traditions with modern medical practices to provide holistic healing experiences.

With over 25 years of experience, we have successfully treated thousands of patients suffering from various chronic conditions using our unique blend of herbal remedies, therapeutic treatments, and lifestyle guidance.

Our team of qualified Ayurvedic physicians and therapists are dedicated to understanding your unique constitution (dosha) and creating personalized treatment plans that address the root cause of your health concerns.

Dr. Nayani Rasnayaka

BAMS, MD (Ayurveda), PhD in Medicinal Plants
Founder & Chief Physician
